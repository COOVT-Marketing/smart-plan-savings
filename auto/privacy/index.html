Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2025 · Applies to auto insurance pages at smartplansavings.site/auto

Smart Plan Savings (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use our auto insurance pages, request a quote, or contact us by phone.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact information – name, phone number, email, ZIP code, state.
  • Quote-related information – number of vehicles, type of need (e.g. SR-22), and other details you provide.
  • Technical data – IP address, browser type, device info, pages visited.
  • Call recordings – calls may be recorded for quality, training, and compliance.

2. How We Use Information

  • Respond to quote requests and connect you with licensed agents.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Comply with legal obligations (including TCPA record-keeping).
  • Protect against fraud and enforce our terms.

3. Sharing

We may share information with licensed insurance agents and agencies that help fulfill your request, service providers under confidentiality obligations, and authorities when required by law. We do not sell your personal information for monetary consideration in the conventional sense. California residents: see our CCPA notice.

4. Consent for Calls and Texts

When you submit a form or provide a phone number and consent, you authorize Smart Plan Savings and its licensed agents to contact you at that number regarding insurance quotes, including via autodialed or prerecorded calls and text messages. Consent is not required to obtain a quote. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to texts or telling the agent.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your state, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or limit use of your personal information. California residents have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA.
