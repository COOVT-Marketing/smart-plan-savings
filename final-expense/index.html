What this kind of policy is for

Immediate costs, not a full estate plan.

Funeral and burial

A modest death benefit meant for funeral home costs, burial or cremation, and related bills.

Leftover medical bills

Hospital or doctor balances that can still arrive after a death, so they are less likely to fall on relatives.

Travel for family

Some families use a portion so children or siblings can get to a service without taking on debt.

Small outstanding debts

Policies are not a catch-all estate plan. They are built for immediate, practical costs.

Plain answers

Is this a government benefit?

No. Final expense life insurance is a private product sold by licensed agents. This site is not a Medicare, Social Security, or federal website, and Smart Plan Savings is not affiliated with the U.S. government.

Do I have to take a medical exam?

Many final expense policies are simplified-issue and do not require an exam. Eligibility still depends on the carrier, your age, health questions, and your state. An agent has to explain what actually applies to you.

What does it cost to use this form?

Nothing. We may be paid by partner agents if you are connected through this site. That does not change the price of a policy the carrier would otherwise charge.

Will I be called or texted?

Only if you check the consent box. That consent covers Smart Plan Savings and the partners listed on the Partner Network page at the version shown in the form. You can revoke it by emailing support@smartplansavings.site

Are you an insurance company?

No. We generate marketing leads and introduce consumers to independently licensed agents. We do not underwrite, bind coverage, or guarantee that any carrier will issue a policy.
