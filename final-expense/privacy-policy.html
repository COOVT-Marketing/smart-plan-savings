Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Smart Plan Savings collects, uses, and shares personal information when you use our websites and forms. It is written for consumers in the United States.

Effective date: January 1, 2025 - Last updated: January 1, 2026

1. Who we are

Smart Plan Savings is a marketing and lead generation company. We are not an insurance carrier, agency, or broker. We operate websites that describe insurance products and connect interested consumers with independently licensed agents in our Partner Network.

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the United States government, CMS, Medicare, Medicaid, Social Security, or any federal or state agency.

Contact: support@smartplansavings.site. Phone: .

2. Information we collect

  • Identifiers you type into a form: name, phone, email, ZIP, state, age range.
  • Consent records, including checkbox state, timestamp, page URL, and partner-list version.
  • Device and usage data: IP address (captured when you load the page and stored with each form submission), browser type, referring URL, UTM parameters, and pages viewed.
  • Lead-certification data from vendors such as TrustedForm (ActiveProspect) and Jornaya (LeadiD).
  • Call recordings if you speak with a partner agent, as disclosed on the form.

We do not ask you to submit Social Security numbers or payment card numbers on this website.

3. How we use information

  • Connect you with licensed agents about the product you inquired about.
  • Honor and document TCPA/consent records.
  • Operate, secure, and improve the website.
  • Measure advertising performance.
  • Comply with law and prevent fraud, spam, and form abuse.

4. How we share information

If you submit a form and provide consent, we share your inquiry with one or more partners listed on the Partner Network page at the version recorded with your submission (currently v1.0.0). Those partners may contact you about insurance options.

We also share information with service providers that host the site, process forms, or certify leads; professional advisers and authorities when required by law; and a successor if we sell or reorganize the business.

We do not sell your information to retailers, data brokers, or marketers unrelated to the insurance inquiry you submitted. Some state laws treat sharing for advertising as a "sale" or "share." See Section 8 and our Do Not Sell or Share page.

5. Calls, texts, and TCPA consent

Checking the consent box is prior express written consent to be contacted by Smart Plan Savings and the listed partners at the number you provide, including autodialed calls, prerecorded or artificial voice, and SMS. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. You may revoke consent by emailing support@smartplansavings.site or using STOP on SMS where that option is offered.

6. Call recording

Calls with agents may be recorded for quality and compliance. If you are in a state that requires all-party consent (including California, Florida, Pennsylvania, Illinois, and Washington), staying on the line is consent to recording.

7. Cookies and similar tools

We and our vendors use cookies, pixels, and scripts for site function, fraud prevention, lead certification (TrustedForm, Jornaya), and advertising measurement. You can block cookies in your browser; the form may still work, but certification or analytics can be incomplete.

8. California and other state privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA and similar)

If you are a resident of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, or another state with a comprehensive privacy law, you may have the right to know/access, correct, delete (subject to legal exceptions), opt out of sale/sharing or targeted advertising, and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Submit a request on the Do Not Sell or Share page or email support@smartplansavings.site with "Privacy Request" in the subject. We will verify your identity before completing certain requests. You may use an authorized agent as allowed by law.

Categories collected in the past 12 months typically include identifiers, commercial information (your insurance inquiry), internet activity, and inferences used to route a lead.

9. Retention

We keep form submissions, consent logs, and lead certificates for as long as needed to connect you with agents, resolve disputes, and meet TCPA, state insurance, and tax record-keeping duties. That period is often several years.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. No website is perfectly secure.

11. Children

This site is for adults considering life insurance, generally ages 45 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date will change. Material changes that affect consent will be reflected on the form and in the partner-list version when required.
